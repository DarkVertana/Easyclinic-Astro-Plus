## 3\. What are the Everyday Problems Faced by Pulmonology Clinics in India?

Pulmonology clinics have unique operational challenges. Here are real, recurring pain points EasyClinic has observed across speciality practices:

### Problem 1: Overbooked Appointments and Chaotic Scheduling

Seasonal pollution waves, smog-related respiratory issues, and peak influenza months cause unpredictable patient loads. Manual scheduling cannot balance variable consultation times for:

- New COPD patients  
    • Acute asthma attacks  
    • Follow-up visits  
    • Spirometry reviews  
    • Treatment plan adjustments

### Problem 2: Paper-Based Medical Records

Pulmonologists need longitudinal patient data, including:

- PFT values  
    • Chest imaging histories  
    • Medication adherence  
    • Comorbidity records  
    • Allergy profiles

Paper files make this impossible at speed, leading to incomplete records and errors.

### Problem 3: Administrative Overload

Doctors lose up to **30 per cent of clinic time** writing notes, editing prescriptions, generating reports, and coordinating with staff.

This not only slows care but also increases burnout.

### Problem 4: Billing Errors and Delayed Payments

Billing in speciality clinics becomes complex due to:

- Procedure-linked billing  
    • Insurance documentation  
    • Diagnostic test mapping  
    • Follow-up package management

Errors reduce revenue and patient trust.

### Problem 5: Compliance Pressure

With NDHM and digitisation mandates rising, doctors must maintain:

- Standardised records  
    • Secure digital storage  
    • Error-free prescriptions  
    • Audit-ready data

Without automation, this becomes overwhelming.
