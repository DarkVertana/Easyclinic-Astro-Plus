## Understanding Healthcare Compliance in Rwanda

Rwanda’s healthcare compliance framework is shaped by several government entities, including:

- Ministry of Health (MoH)
- Rwanda Biomedical Center (RBC)
- Health professional councils
- District health offices

These bodies enforce protocols covering:

- Patient rights and informed consent
- Recordkeeping and data privacy
- Billing and insurance standards (especially under CBHI)
- Facility infrastructure and sanitation
- Licensing and continuing professional development (CPD)

Keeping up with these evolving **healthcare standards** manually is time-consuming and error-prone — which is why automation is vital.
