## Understanding NDHM Compliance in India

[NDHM Compliance](https://abdm.gov.in/) in India is built on a national digital health architecture designed to connect patients, providers, and payers through standardised, consent-based data exchange.

At its foundation are five interconnected pillars:

- ABHA Health ID for patient identification

- Personal Health Records for longitudinal care history

- Health Facility Registry for verified institutions

- Healthcare Professionals Registry for verified providers

- Secure APIs and interoperability standards for data exchange

To remain compliant, clinics must digitise records, adopt structured data formats, respect consent protocols, and ensure data security at every level.
