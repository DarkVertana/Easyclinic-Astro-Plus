### The Growing Demand for Rural Telehealth in Africa

**Accessibility Challenges in Rural Clinics**

Many rural clinics in Africa face persistent hurdles:

- Scarcity of specialists
- Poor infrastructure
- Long travel distances for patients

These issues impact not just patient outcomes but also increase operational pressure on local practitioners.

**Rural telehealth** helps eliminate these barriers by offering remote consultations via mobile devices, laptops, or clinic terminals. EasyClinic takes it a step further—its telemedicine feature is built-in and designed for low-bandwidth environments, perfect for rural clinics.
