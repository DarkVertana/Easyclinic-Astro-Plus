## 3\. The Everyday Problems Faced by Immunology Clinics

Based on EasyClinic’s experience supporting speciality practices, the following challenges are common in immunology clinics across India.

### Overbooked appointments and chaotic scheduling

Skin prick tests, patch tests, immunotherapy sessions, and biologic infusions require carefully timed slots. Walk-ins, flare-ups, and unpredictable follow-ups create scheduling chaos when handled manually.

### Paper-based patient records slow down decisions.

Patients with autoimmune disorders often require long-term monitoring of biomarkers, treatment history, flare patterns, and medication responses. Manual records make it difficult to track progression or retrieve past data rapidly.

### Administrative overload reduces clinical focus.

Doctors spend hours reviewing test reports, updating treatment logs, planning immunotherapy cycles, and coordinating with laboratory teams. Administrative pressure reduces time available for patient counselling and personalised intervention.

### Financial inconsistencies and delayed billing

Billing for allergy tests, immunotherapy vials, biologic injections, follow-up consultations, and diagnostic reviews becomes complicated without automated systems.

### Compliance and documentation challenges

Accurate record keeping is essential for chronic care. Paper-based workflows risk missing data, lost files, and errors in therapy documentation.

Imagine an immunology clinic in Hyderabad treating patients with asthma, eczema, psoriasis, rheumatoid arthritis, food allergies, and chronic urticaria. Without digital systems, tracking IgE levels, ANA trends, medication adjustments, and therapy progress becomes overwhelming for both staff and clinicians.
