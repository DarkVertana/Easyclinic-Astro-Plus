## The Critical Role of Community Health Workers in Rwanda

Rwanda’s CHW program is globally recognised for its structure and reach. With tens of thousands of trained workers embedded within communities, CHWs are responsible for:

• Household-level disease surveillance  
• Maternal and child health follow-ups  
• Nutrition and immunisation tracking  
• Early referrals for malaria, pneumonia, and chronic illness  
• Health education and behavioural change communication

Despite this responsibility, limited tools restrict their impact. Paper forms delay reporting, data inconsistencies affect referrals, and supervisors lack real-time visibility. **AI EMR Solutions Rwanda** address these gaps at scale.

AI EMR Solutions Rwanda enable seamless continuity by ensuring that patient records created at the community level follow the patient throughout the healthcare system. This continuity reduces duplication of tests, improves diagnostic accuracy, and ensures that treatment plans remain consistent even as patients transition between care providers.
