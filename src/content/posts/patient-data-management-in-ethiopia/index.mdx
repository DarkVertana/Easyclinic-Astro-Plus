## The Challenge: Disconnected Data Systems in Ethiopia

Ethiopia’s healthcare system, while expanding in reach, continues to struggle with:

- Fragmented patient records across multiple facilities
- Lost or illegible paper files
- Delays in accessing lab reports or referral notes
- Limited documentation during field visits

These issues compromise continuity of care, especially for chronic conditions, maternal health, and vaccinations.

### What is Mobile EMR and Why It Matters

A **mobile EMR** enables doctors, nurses, and health workers to:

- View and update patient records from a phone or tablet
- Record vitals, diagnosis, and medications on the go
- Access historical data, labs, and prescriptions in real time

In resource-limited settings, this improves clinical workflows, even in the absence of a desktop or consistent internet access.
