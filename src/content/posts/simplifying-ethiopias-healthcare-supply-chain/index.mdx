## The Crisis Behind the Counter: Ethiopia’s Healthcare Supply Chain Issues

## Ethiopia faces long-standing inefficiencies driven by:

• Frequent drug and vaccine stockouts  
• Overstocking of slow-moving items due to inaccurate forecasts  
• Expired inventory accumulating unnoticed  
• Delayed procurement from the central medical stores  
• Poor visibility for district and zonal health offices  
• Weak last-mile delivery data  
• Manual stock cards are prone to human error

These gaps trickle down into treatment inequality, dissatisfied patients, and financial loss for clinics trying to stretch every birr. **A strong Healthcare Supply Chain is not optional — it is the foundation of reliable, equitable care.**
