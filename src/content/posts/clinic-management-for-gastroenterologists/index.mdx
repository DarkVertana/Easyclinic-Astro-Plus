Despite the advances in diagnostics, many gastroenterology clinics continue to face day-to-day operational challenges that directly impact patient outcomes and clinic revenue.

1. **Chaotic appointment scheduling**Endoscopy and colonoscopy procedures require time blocks, prep coordination, and post-recovery observation — yet many clinics still manage bookings manually. This leads to double-bookings and idle hours.
2. **Paper-based or fragmented patient records**Diagnostic reports, pathology results, and endoscopic images often exist on separate systems or hard drives, making retrieval slow and prone to error.
