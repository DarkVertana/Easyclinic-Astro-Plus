## Implementing a Staff Training Strategy

### Step 1: Assess Skill Gaps

Use staff performance reviews and patient feedback to identify areas needing improvement. Break down training needs by department and role.

### Step 2: Develop a Training Plan

Build a 3–6-month plan with:

- Induction modules for new hires
- Monthly sessions on specific topics
- Online training resources and checklists
- Periodic assessments

### Step 3: Assign Trainers and Mentors

Identify senior team members who can mentor junior staff. Rotate training responsibilities to share knowledge and avoid burnout.

### Step 4: Use Tools and Software for Delivery

Use digital tools to simplify training:

- Recorded webinars
- Step-by-step walkthroughs in EMR or billing software
- Online quizzes and progress tracking

### Step 5: Monitor Progress and Offer Feedback

Track attendance, assess comprehension, and provide feedback.

Reward well-performing staff through:

- Certificates
- Role upgrades
- Bonuses or incentives
