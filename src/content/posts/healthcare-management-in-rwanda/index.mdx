## Rwanda’s Ambitious Journey Toward Universal Healthcare Coverage

Rwanda’s path toward universal healthcare has been shaped by several groundbreaking decisions:

● The introduction of CBHI (Mutuelle de Santé)  
● Decentralised district-level health facility management  
● Performance-based financing tied to quality  
● Strategic partnerships with development agencies  
● National eHealth and digital transformation strategy

The country’s health system operates on a clearly structured tiered model:

1. **Health Posts** – Serving villages and rural catchment areas

2. **Health Centres** – Primary-level outpatient services

3. **District Hospitals** – Secondary care and emergency services

4. **Referral Hospitals** – Complex and specialised care

This hierarchy makes healthcare accessible, but also creates challenges:

● Tracking patient movement between facilities  
● Ensuring continuity of care  
● Processing CBHI insurance categories correctly  
● Avoiding duplication of patient records  
● Ensuring rural clinics communicate with urban hospitals  
● Maintaining accurate national dashboards

Universal healthcare coverage depends on more than broad policies — it depends on smooth workflows, accurate billing, reliable digital tools, and strong **healthcare management** systems.
