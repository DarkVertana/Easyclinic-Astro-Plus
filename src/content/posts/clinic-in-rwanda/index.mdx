## Obtain Licenses to Operate Legally

Licenses legitimise **starting a clinic in Rwanda**. Multiple approvals ensure your clinic meets national health and safety standards.

- **Professional License**: RMDC approval costs RWF50,000–RWF100,000 per year, verifying your credentials to practice medicine legally.

- **Facility License**: Meet infrastructure standards for RWF200,000–RWF500,000, ensuring space and equipment meet health guidelines.
- **REMA**: Environmental permit costs RWF100,000–RWF300,000, covering waste management and ecological compliance.
- **Renewal**: Annual checks are required, aligning with HSSP V guidelines to maintain ongoing compliance.
