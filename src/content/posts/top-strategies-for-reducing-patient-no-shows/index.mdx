A no-show is a patient who booked a slot and neither came nor called. The slot sits empty while someone else waited for a later one, and a missed follow-up can cost the patient more than it costs the clinic. The steps below are the ones a front desk can put in place this month. We give no percentage for how far they will cut your no-shows, because Easy Clinic has not published a measured one.

## Count your no-shows for four weeks first

Before you change anything, find out where the misses happen. Mark every missed appointment as a no-show in the book or the software, rather than deleting it, and note late cancellations separately. For four weeks, count them by:

- day of the week and time of day
- doctor
- new or returning patient
- how far ahead the appointment was booked

The pattern tells you where to start. If the misses cluster in the first slot of the morning, the slot time may be the problem. If they cluster in follow-ups booked weeks ahead, look at the reminders first. The count also gives you the number to measure every later change against. For what a handful of empty slots a day adds up to over a month, the [appointment scheduling page](/features/appointment-scheduling/#what-no-shows-cost) works through an example in rupees and shillings.

## Remind every booked patient, twice

Send one reminder the day before and a short one two to four hours before the visit. The day-before message leaves time to give the slot to another patient. The same-day message catches the patient who simply forgot.

A reminder should carry the doctor's name, the date and time, the clinic's address with a map link, and how to cancel or move the appointment. Send it on the channel the patient reads. SMS reaches any mobile phone, including one without internet, and WhatsApp suits the patients who use it every day.

On Easy Clinic, SMS and email reminders come with every plan, and WhatsApp reminders start on Premium. The reminders go out on their own, days or hours before the visit as the clinic chooses, so nobody at the desk has to send them between patients.

## Make it easy to confirm or move the slot

A reminder the patient only reads tells you nothing about whether they will come. Ask for an answer: a reply, or a call if they cannot make it. Then the desk can spend its calls on the patients who have not answered, in the hour before their session starts. Easy Clinic tracks which patients have acknowledged their reminder, so the desk can see who to call.

Offer to move the appointment rather than cancel it. "If you cannot come, call us and we will find you another time" keeps the patient, and frees the slot for someone else in time to use it. Say in the first message how much notice you need to move a slot, and put the same line on the appointment card and the clinic's website.

## Four messages around each booking

A reminder is one message of four. The other three answer the questions that can keep a patient away, and bring back the one who missed.

| Message | When it goes | What it says |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Confirmation | When the appointment is booked | The date and time, the doctor, the clinic's address and how to move the appointment |
| Preparation | The day before | What to bring, such as old reports and the medicines the patient takes, and how early to arrive |
| Reminder | Two to four hours before | The time, and how to tell the clinic they are running late or cannot come |
| Recovery | The same day, after a missed slot | That the clinic missed them, and an offer of another time |

The preparation message is written for first-time patients, who may not know where the clinic is or what happens at the first visit. The recovery message is written without blame. A patient who is running late and embarrassed to call is easier to keep if the reminder tells them how to say so.

Whatever software you use, check which of the four it sends on its own and which the desk has to send by hand. If you plan to send them on WhatsApp, the [WhatsApp page](/features/whatsapp/) covers how message templates work.

## Fill the slots that still open

- **Keep a waitlist.** Note the patients who asked for an earlier slot, and call them when someone moves.
- **Use walk-ins.** On Easy Clinic, walk-ins join the same calendar as booked appointments on every plan, so a slot that opens can go to a patient already waiting.
- **Overbook with care.** Overbook only the slots where your four-week count shows a patient missing on most days, and by one patient at most. Otherwise the patients who came on time wait longer.
- **Offer video for suitable follow-ups.** For a follow-up that does not need an examination, a video consult saves the patient the journey. Video consults need Easy Clinic's Premium plan, and the [telehealth page](/features/telehealth/) shows how they are booked.

## Call the patient who did not come

Call on the same day. Ask whether they are all right and offer a new time. Keep the call short and without blame, because the aim is the next booking.

Write down the reason they give. Over a month, the reasons tell you whether to change the slot times or the reminders. A patient who misses the same early slot twice may keep an evening one, if your doctors have one to offer.

## What reminders will not do

Reminders help the patient who forgot. They do nothing for a patient who decided not to come, whether because of the cost or because they feel better, and that patient needs the call, not another message. Measure the result against your own four-week count rather than against a figure from someone else's clinic.
