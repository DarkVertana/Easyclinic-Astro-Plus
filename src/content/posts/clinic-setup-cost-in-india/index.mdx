This post works through a clinic budget for India in the order the bills arrive. It uses one worked example, Easy Clinic's budget for a small clinic in Mumbai, and current listings for rent and equipment, each with the date we checked it. Every figure will have moved by the time you read it, so use them to check your own quotes, not in place of them.

## Where the money goes

Pricing a clinic item by item gets the couch and the desk right, and misses the lines paid before the first patient. The Mumbai budget, for a 500-square-foot general clinic, splits like this:

## Rent and the deposit

Rent varies sharply from one city to the next. On 26 September 2026, [MagicBricks](https://www.magicbricks.com/office-space-for-rent-in-vadodara-pppfr) listed 551 office spaces for rent in Vadodara, from ₹9,900 to ₹1.8 lakh a month. Two listings the size of a one-doctor clinic show the spread: 270 square feet of carpet area on Gotri Road, furnished, at ₹15,000 a month, and a 900-square-foot bare shell in Akota at ₹45,000. The Mumbai budget assumes ₹50,000 a month in an affordable area such as Thane.

Budget the deposit as its own line. It is cash you hand over before you open and usually see again only when you leave. In the Mumbai budget it runs from ₹50,000 in Thane to ₹3,00,000 in Bandra.

Fit-out follows the rent. The Mumbai budget puts ₹3,00,000 into the interiors of 500 square feet and ₹1,00,000 into furniture for the waiting room and a consultation room. The Vadodara listings mix bare-shell units with furnished ones, and a bare shell costs more to fit out. Compare units on rent plus fit-out, and visit each one at the hours your patients would come.

## Equipment: what current listings show

Equipment depends on your specialty, so price your own list. Two listings, checked on 26 September 2026:

- **Examination table or couch.** [Tradeindia's listings](https://www.tradeindia.com/manufacturers/gynae-examination-couch.html) put most plain examination tables and couches between ₹5,000 and ₹20,000, and motorised or electric couches at ₹38,000 to ₹42,000.
- **ECG machine.** [Bajaj Finance's price list](https://www.bajajfinserv.in/ecg-machine-price-list-in-india), updated on 10 August 2026, gives ₹22,411 to ₹55,885 for a 3-channel machine and ₹32,000 to more than ₹1.3 lakh for a 12-channel one.

Any clinic that reuses instruments needs to sterilise them, so get two quotes for an autoclave sized to your instrument load. Add the pieces your specialty needs after that, and leave the expensive diagnostics until patient numbers justify them. If you plan an X-ray machine, read the licensing section below before you order it.

## Registration and compliance

### Clinical Establishments Act registration

The [Clinical Establishments (Registration and Regulation) Act, 2010](https://clinicalestablishments.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/2021-12/969.pdf) says nobody may run a clinical establishment unless it is registered (section 11). It applies where a state has adopted it. The [Act's official site](https://clinicalestablishments.mohfw.gov.in/) lists Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Bihar, Haryana, Himachal Pradesh, Jharkhand, Mizoram, Rajasthan, Sikkim, Telangana, Uttar Pradesh and Uttarakhand, and every union territory except Delhi. If your state is not on that list, ask the state health department which registration applies to you.

The fee is set state by state. The [fee table on the Act's site](https://clinicalestablishments.mohfw.gov.in/sites/default/files/2023-06/46311.pdf) lists ₹100 for a clinic's provisional registration and ₹500 for permanent registration in Chandigarh, and ₹500 for a clinic in Jharkhand. The fee is only part of it: under section 12 a clinic must meet the minimum standards for facilities and services, the minimum staffing, the rules on keeping records and reporting, and any other conditions the government prescribes.

A few dates for the diary. The authority issues a provisional certificate within ten days of receiving the application (section 15), and it is valid until the last day of the twelfth month after it is issued (section 17). A clinic set up after the Act came into force applies for permanent registration within six months of its establishment (section 14(4)). The certificate goes on the wall where patients can see it (section 18).

### Biomedical waste

If the clinic throws away used needles or soiled dressings, it produces biomedical waste. The Central Pollution Control Board's [guidelines on the Bio-Medical Waste Management Rules, 2016](https://cpcb.nic.in/uploads/projects/bio-medical-waste/guidelines_healthcare_june_2018.pdf) name clinics among the occupiers the rules cover. Each facility applies to the State Pollution Control Board for authorisation. A facility within 75 km of a common biomedical waste treatment facility needs a valid agreement with it. The facility files an annual report with the Board by 30 June, and one with fewer than 30 beds keeps the receipts the treatment facility prints.

The agreement is a recurring bill, so it belongs in running costs. Ask the treatment facility that serves your area for its rate before you finish the budget.

### X-ray equipment

An X-ray machine brings a regulator of its own. The [Atomic Energy Regulatory Board](https://aerb.gov.in/english/regulatory-facilities/radiation-facilities/application-in-medicine/diagnostic-radiology) says Rule 3 of the Atomic Energy (Radiation Protection) Rules, 2004 makes a licence mandatory for every user of X-ray equipment, applied for through its e-LORA system. AERB has warned that facilities found using X-ray equipment without a licence will be sealed, and a licensed facility displays its licence. Put the licence in the plan before you pay for the machine.

### The rest of the list

The doctor's own registration is covered in the [NMC and council registration guide](/how-to-get-approval-from-the-medical-practitioners-and-dentists-council-in-india-nmc-dci/), and [how to start a clinic in India](/clinic-in-india/) walks through the local licences in order. Ask each office for its current fee before you fill in the licences line.

## Running costs to hold back

Rent and salaries are due every month from the start. Patients take longer to arrive. The Mumbai budget holds back three months: ₹1,50,000 of rent, ₹2,70,000 for three staff at ₹30,000 a month each, ₹45,000 for utilities and ₹45,000 for maintenance.

Three months is the least we would plan for. If the area is new to you, or the clinic depends on referrals that have not started yet, hold back more.
