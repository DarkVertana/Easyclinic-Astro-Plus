## 1\. Introduction: A New Era for Autoimmune and Arthritis Care in India

In India, rheumatology clinics play an increasingly critical role in supporting patients with chronic autoimmune and musculoskeletal disorders. With conditions such as rheumatoid arthritis, lupus, ankylosing spondylitis, psoriatic arthritis, and osteoarthritis rising consistently across Indian demographics, the country now faces a significant challenge. According to multiple Indian Council of Medical Research insights, autoimmune disorders often go undiagnosed for months, leading to delayed treatment and higher long-term disability.

Rheumatology clinics, therefore, manage some of the most complex and chronic patient cases. These patients require continuous monitoring, detailed documentation, long-term treatment adherence, and structured follow-up cycles. The care burden is high, and administrative pressure continues to grow as patient volumes increase.
