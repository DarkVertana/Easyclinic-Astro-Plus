## 2\. Understanding NHIF Empanelment Requirements

Before applying to **get empanelled with NHIF in Kenya**, you need to meet specific requirements set by **NHIF** and regulatory bodies. These ensure your **medical clinic in Kenya** adheres to quality and legal standards:

### Licensing by Regulatory Bodies

- Obtain a valid license from the **Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC)** to operate as a healthcare facility.
- For pharmacies (KEPH Level 3A and above), secure a license from the **Pharmacy and Poisons Board**.
- For laboratories, get a license from the **Kenya Medical Laboratory Technicians and Technologists Board**.
- Oncology providers need a certificate from the **National Cancer Institute of Kenya**.

### Business Registration

- Register your clinic as a business with the **Business Registration Service**.
- Obtain a **CR12** (for incorporated facilities) or **CR13** (for business name registration).

### Quality Standards

- Complete the **NHIF Self-Assessment Quality Checklist** to evaluate your clinic’s readiness.
- For dialysis units, have a **Service Level Agreement (SLA)** with an **NHIF-accredited hospital** with ICU/HDU facilities.

Meeting these requirements ensures your **healthcare clinic in Kenya** is eligible for **NHIF empanelment** and can deliver trusted **healthcare in Kenya**.

## 3\. Step-by-Step Process to Get Empanelled with NHIF in Kenya

The process to **get empanelled with NHIF in Kenya** involves several steps, from licensing to contracting. Follow these steps to ensure your **private clinic in Kenya** becomes an **NHIF-accredited provider**:

### Step 1: Obtain Necessary Licenses

- Apply for a license from the **Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC)** to operate your clinic legally.
- Secure additional licenses if applicable:
    - **Pharmacy and Poisons Board** for pharmacies.
    - **Kenya Medical Laboratory Technicians and Technologists Board** for labs.
    - **National Cancer Institute, Kenya,** for oncology services.

### Step 2: Register Your Business

- Register your clinic with the **Business Registration Service**.
- Obtain a **CR12** (for companies) or **CR13** (for business names).
- If your clinic’s name has changed, provide a **Certificate of Change of Name**.

### Step 3: Apply to NHIF

- Visit the [NHIF website](https://www.nhif.or.ke) and download the empanelment application form.
- Submit the completed form to your nearest **NHIF branch**.
- **NHIF** will verify your application through its branch network, checking your licenses and facility details.

### Step 4: NHIF Board Approval

- After verification, your application goes to the **NHIF Board** for review.
- The Board evaluates your clinic’s compliance with **NHIF standards** and approves empanelment if all requirements are met.

### Step 5: Gazettement

- Once approved, your clinic is **gazetted** at the **Attorney General’s Office**.
- Gazettement is the official announcement of your empanelment, making it legally recognised.

### Step 6: Contracting

- **NHIF** will draft a contract outlining the terms of empanelment, including tariffs for services.
- Review the contract carefully (available on the [NHIF website](https://www.nhif.or.ke)) and sign it to finalise empanelment.
- The contract specifies payment rates for benefits like outpatient care, maternity, or dialysis.

By following these steps, your clinic can successfully **get empanelled with NHIF in Kenya** and start serving insured patients in the **health sector in Kenya**.

## 4\. Documents Needed for NHIF Empanelment

To apply for **NHIF empanelment**, you’ll need to submit the following documents to your **NHIF branch**:

- **Certified Copy of KMPDC Registration Certificate:** Proof of registration with the **Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council**.
- **Certified Copy of KMPDC Operating License:** A valid license to operate your clinic, issued by **KMPDC**.
- **Certified Copy of Business Registration:** Either a **Certificate of Incorporation** or **Certificate of Business Name**, along with a **CR12** or **CR13** form.
- **NHIF Self-Assessment Quality Checklist:** Download this from the [NHIF website](https://www.nhif.or.ke) (under “Downloads”) and complete it to show your clinic’s readiness.
- **Service Level Agreement (SLA):** For standalone dialysis units, an SLA with an **NHIF-accredited hospital** with ICU/HDU facilities.
- **Pharmacy License:** For KEPH Level 3A and above facilities, a certificate and up-to-date license from the **Pharmacy and Poisons Board**.
- **Laboratory License:** A certificate and up-to-date license from the **Kenya Medical Laboratory Technicians and Technologists Board**.
- **Oncology Certificate:** For oncology providers, a certificate from the **National Cancer Institute, Kenya**.
- **Certificate of Change of Name:** If your clinic’s name has changed, include this document.

Ensure all documents are certified as true copies and submitted in the required format. Proper documentation is key to getting **empanelled with NHIF in Kenya**.
