This guide is for a doctor planning to open a private medical clinic in Malaysia. It follows the Ministry of Health's own documents, checked on 26 September 2026: the CKAPS guideline for processing clinic registrations, dated 2 September 2024, its checklist, the guidebook the Ministry published with the Malaysia Productivity Corporation in March 2024, and the Malaysian Medical Council's page on practising certificates. Where we found no official figure, such as a total setup cost, the guide says so rather than guessing.

## Clinics are registered with CKAPS

Private clinics come under the Private Healthcare Facilities and Services Act 1998 (Act 586) and its 2006 regulations for private medical and dental clinics, in force since 1 May 2006. [The CKAPS guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf) says every private medical clinic must comply with both before it can be registered and start operating.

The word that matters is registered. [CKAPS lists private medical and dental clinics under registration](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/klinik-swasta/), and hospitals and the other larger facilities under licensing. What your clinic receives at the end is a certificate of registration, called Borang B for a medical clinic.

CKAPS is the Private Medical Practice Control Section of the Ministry's Medical Practice Division. Its headquarters in Putrajaya processes the application, and its office in each state health department (CKAPS JKN/WP) gives the pre-registration consultation and carries out the inspection.

## Before you apply: your own registration

The clinic's registration rests on yours, so start here.

- Full registration with the MMC, and an Annual Practicing Certificate (APC) for the current year. [The MMC](https://mmc.gov.my/annual-practicing-certificates-apc/) says the Medical Act 1971 requires the APC for each year you practise, and that the application needs professional indemnity cover and at least 20 CPD points. The APC for 2027 can be renewed without a late fee until 30 November 2026.
- Compulsory service. The person in charge of a general clinic must have completed it. If yours was shorter than two years, [the guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf) asks for the Ministry's letter approving the exemption or reduction.
- An APC that names the clinic. The APC you submit must show the name and address of the clinic you are registering. If it still shows another private facility, apply to the MMC to change it. If it shows a Ministry facility, attach the letter confirming your resignation.
- You cannot apply while you serve with the Ministry of Health. Doctors in other ministries, such as a university hospital, need their head of department's written permission, and the clinic's hours must fall outside their working hours.
- One clinic each. A registered practitioner can be the person in charge of one private clinic only.

Under [the guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf), a specialist clinic needs a person in charge with the matching specialist qualification and a National Specialist Register certificate, and it may offer only that specialty. A specialist cannot be the person in charge of a general clinic, with the exceptions the guideline lists, such as a specialist who no longer wants to practise the specialty.

## Decide how the business is held

The [guidebook](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NEW_MPC_GUIDEBOOK_230324NEW_FINAL_FAOL_1-1.pdf) sets out the choices and what SSM charges.

| Structure | SSM registration | Fee in the guidebook |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sole proprietor | Not needed for sole ownership in professional practice | None |
| Company limited by shares | Yes, under the Companies Act 2016 | RM1,000 |
| Company limited by guarantee | Yes, under the Companies Act 2016 | RM3,000 |
| Limited liability partnership | Yes, under the LLP Act 2012 | RM500 |

The sole proprietor exemption comes from [section 4(d) of the Registration of Businesses Act 1956](https://www.ssm.com.my/acts/fscommand/a0197s0004.htm), which does not apply to a business that consists only of a profession whose members must be registered under a written law. For a company, the guidebook asks that at least one director is a registered medical practitioner with a valid APC. For an LLP, the partners must be people practising the same profession.

Whichever structure you choose, the certificate of registration for a medical clinic goes to an individual registered medical practitioner. The CKAPS guideline cites subsection 7(2) of Act 586 for this.

## Choose premises that will pass the floor plan check

Most applications stand or fall on the floor plan, so check the premises against these rules from [the CKAPS guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf) before you sign a tenancy.

- The premises must be on a commercial lot, not a residential lot or private land.
- The plan is drawn at 1:100 by at least a draughtsman, with the columns and beams shown, every room labelled and the measurements taken wall to wall.
- One consultation room for each doctor who practises there.
- Every patient route has a clear opening of at least 1.2 m, and the ramp has a gradient no steeper than 1:12.
- Above the ground floor, the stairs must be at least 1.1 m wide and the door to the stairs 1.2 m clear, under the Uniform Building By-Laws.
- The building needs a Certificate of Fitness or a Certificate of Completion and Compliance, or a receipt for assessment tax paid to the local authority in its place.
- Above the 12th storey, or more than 48 m above ground, [the checklist](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Senarai-Semak-Pendaftaran-PMC-ver-Sept-2024-1.pdf) also asks for a letter of support from the Fire and Rescue Department (Bomba).

The clinic's name has rules too, in [the same guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf). It must match on Borang A, the signboard, the APC and the certificate. CKAPS suggests names such as Klinik Perubatan Dr. Tan. It refuses names that sound like a Ministry facility (Klinik Kesihatan, Klinik Desa), names that mislead about the services (centre, wellness), aesthetic words (spa, beauty), names that are too narrow (Klinik Diabetes) and names that overclaim (international, the best).

In Sarawak and Sabah there is more to do. [The guidebook](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/NEW_MPC_GUIDEBOOK_230324NEW_FINAL_FAOL_1-1.pdf) has a section on immigration requirements for doctors who are not residents of those states and on state and local authority rules, and [the checklist](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Senarai-Semak-Pendaftaran-PMC-ver-Sept-2024-1.pdf) asks for building plan approval in Sarawak.

## The registration route, step by step

This is the procedure in force since 2 September 2024, from the [flowchart the Ministry published with it](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Surat-KPK-Pemakluman-Prosedur-Kerja-Pendaftaran-Klinik_260824.pdf).

1. Read the guidelines on the Medical Practice Division website and contact your state CKAPS office for a pre-registration consultation.
2. Fill in Borang A online in MyMedPCs, print one copy and sign it by hand, because scanned signatures are not accepted. Send it with the supporting documents and the RM500 processing fee to CKAPS HQ. The fee is paid by money order or bank draft to the Ketua Setiausaha Kementerian Kesihatan Malaysia.
3. CKAPS HQ works through applications in a queue. Answer its queries until the application complies.
4. An evaluation committee endorses the application once the floor plan complies.
5. CKAPS HQ sends a no-objection letter for the renovation.
6. Renovate to the approved plan within three months. If you need longer, ask CKAPS HQ in writing for an extension; if the work cannot be finished in the time allowed, the application has to be withdrawn and made again.
7. Send CKAPS HQ the notification that the renovation is complete, with your undertaking.
8. CKAPS HQ sends a letter asking for the issuance fee, with a document called Format A that shows the details to be printed on the certificate. Check Format A, then send it back with the fee, which is RM1,000 on the fee slip in [the CKAPS checklist](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Senarai-Semak-Pendaftaran-PMC-ver-Sept-2024-1.pdf).
9. Receive the certificate of registration. You may start operating.
10. The state CKAPS office visits to inspect the clinic against the standards.

## Put the document pack together

[The checklist](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Senarai-Semak-Pendaftaran-PMC-ver-Sept-2024-1.pdf) and [the guideline](https://hq.moh.gov.my/medicalprac/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/GARIS-PANDUAN-PENDAFTARAN-KLINIK_SEPTEMBER-2024.pdf) ask for these, among others, with copies certified by a government officer of grade 41 or above, a medical officer, a judicial or legal officer, or an officer of equivalent standing. Work through the checklist itself before you send anything, because it is the list CKAPS checks against.

- Borang A, printed from MyMedPCs and signed by hand, and the RM500 processing fee.
- A certified copy of your identity card.
- An original statutory declaration, not more than two years old, signed before a Sessions Court judge, a magistrate, a Commissioner for Oaths or a notary public. It states that you have not been convicted of an offence involving fraud or dishonesty and are not an undischarged bankrupt, and it cannot be one made for a company.
- Certified copies of your full registration certificate, your qualification certificates and the APC naming the clinic.
- A letter of consent from the person in charge, if that is not you.
- The SSM corporate information for a company, or the current profile for an LLP.
- The Certificate of Fitness or Certificate of Completion and Compliance, or the assessment tax receipt.
- Photographs of the site from the front and the back.
- A current clinical waste disposal agreement and a current vector control agreement, each showing the clinic's name and address.
- For each doctor, nurse and other health professional on the staff, certified copies of their identity card, full registration and current APC, and a certified copy of each support worker's identity card.
- A statement of what it cost to set up the clinic. A copy of the bank book is not needed.
- The floor plan, and the artwork for the signboard, which follows its own CKAPS guideline.
- The list of services you will offer, all within outpatient care.
- The autoclave's certificate of fitness, or the first inspection report from the Department of Occupational Safety and Health (JKKP) if the certificate has not come yet.
- For X-ray, the atomic energy licence stamped for storing and use, after approval from the Ministry's medical radiation regulator (BKRP).
