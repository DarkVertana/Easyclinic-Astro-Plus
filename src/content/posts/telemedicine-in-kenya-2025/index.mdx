## 3\. Types of Telemedicine for Clinics in Kenya

Understanding the types of telemedicine can help you choose the best approach for your **healthcare clinic in Kenya**. There are three main types, categorised by the timing of information exchange:

**Store-and-Forward**  
This method involves storing patient data, such as test results or medical images, and transmitting it to a healthcare provider for later review. There’s no real-time interaction, making it ideal for non-urgent cases like dermatology consultations at your **medical clinic in Kenya**.

**Remote Monitoring**  
Remote monitoring uses technological devices to track a patient’s health remotely, often for chronic conditions like diabetes or cardiovascular disease. This can help your **private clinic in Kenya** manage patients with ongoing needs in healthcare needs.
