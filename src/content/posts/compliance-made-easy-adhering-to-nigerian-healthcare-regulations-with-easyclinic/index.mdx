**Understanding the Healthcare Compliance Landscape in Nigeria**

Healthcare regulation in Nigeria is overseen by various bodies including the Medical and Dental Council of Nigeria (MDCN), National Health Insurance Authority (NHIA), and state health ministries. Clinics are required to adhere to rules surrounding:

- Licensing and staff qualifications
- Documentation of patient encounters
- Accurate medical billing and insurance processes
- Patient confidentiality (data privacy laws)
- Facility standards and operational protocols

Failure to comply may lead to fines, revocation of licenses, or even legal action. Yet many clinics, particularly smaller ones, struggle due to outdated paper systems and staff unfamiliar with regulatory procedures.
