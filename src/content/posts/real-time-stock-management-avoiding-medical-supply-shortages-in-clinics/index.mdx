## Why Real-Time Inventory Matters in Healthcare

In clinical settings, delays in replenishing gloves, syringes, or diagnostic tools can lead to postponed procedures or compromised care. **Real-time inventory** systems:

- Monitor supply levels continuously
- Provide up-to-the-minute stock visibility
- Prevent usage of expired or unsafe stock
- Enable predictive supply planning based on consumption trends
