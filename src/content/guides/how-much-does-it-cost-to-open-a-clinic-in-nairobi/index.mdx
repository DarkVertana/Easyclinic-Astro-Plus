## The budget for a small clinic, line by line

A small general clinic in Nairobi comes to about KES 3.2 million if you build it yourself. That figure assumes a 100-square-metre building on a plot in one of the city's more affordable areas, a Level 2 licence, three staff and the first three months of running costs.

| Item | KES | Basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Construction | 1,800,000 | 100 square metres at KES 18,000 per square metre |
| Land | 500,000 | A small plot in an affordable area such as Eastlands |
| Building permits | 9,000 | About 0.5% of the construction cost |
| Utility connections | 75,000 | Electricity and water, from a range of KES 50,000 to 100,000 |
| Registration and licensing | 26,000 | Level 2 clinic |
| Rent, 3 months | 90,000 | KES 30,000 a month |
| Utilities, 3 months | 30,000 | KES 10,000 a month, internet included |
| Staff salaries, 3 months | 285,000 | KES 95,000 a month for three staff at the bottom of each pay range |
| Equipment | 300,000 | Basic examination equipment |
| Supplies, 3 months | 60,000 | KES 20,000 a month |
| Insurance and miscellaneous | 70,000 | Indemnity cover plus signage and marketing |
| **Total** | **About 3.2 million** | Every line above |

Read the total with two caveats. It counts both a new building and three months of rent, so if you rent a finished space the construction and land lines drop out, and if you build, the rent line does. And most lines sit at the low end of their ranges, so each is more likely to rise than fall when the quotes come in.

Fee schedules change and this page can lag behind them. Check each council fee on the council's own site before you pay.

## Building the clinic

### What moves the building cost

Four things decide what a clinic building costs in Nairobi:

- Location. Land and building costs are higher in the city because demand is higher.
- Size and type. A small general clinic costs less than a specialised facility such as an eye or maternity clinic.
- Access to utilities. A plot that still needs utilities or a road connection costs more to bring into use.
- Materials and labour. Local material prices and labour rates set the rest.

### Land and construction

At KES 18,000 per square metre, a common building rate in Nairobi, a basic 100-square-metre clinic costs KES 1.8 million to put up. That is the building alone.

A small plot costs anywhere from KES 500,000 to KES 2 million. The more affordable areas such as Eastlands sit at the bottom of that range and upscale Westlands at the top, so the plot moves your budget more than any fee in this guide.

### County permits and utility connections

Building approval comes from [Nairobi County](https://www.nairobi.go.ke/), and the permits cost about 0.5% of the construction value. On a KES 1.8 million building that is about KES 9,000. Connecting electricity and water costs another KES 50,000 to 100,000; the budget above uses KES 75,000.

## Registration and licensing fees

Two councils appear in this part of the budget. A clinical officer opening a clinic deals with the Clinical Officers Council, and a medical clinic needs a health facility licence from the Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council.

### Clinical Officers Council requirements

To open a private clinic as a clinical officer, the [Clinical Officers Council (COC)](https://www.coc.go.ke/) asks that you:

- are a registered clinical officer with a valid practising licence
- have three years of experience under a senior clinical officer or a medical officer, with a recommendation letter to show it
- have registered your business name with the Registrar of Companies on eCitizen, which costs KES 1,000
- have had your premises inspected by the Sub-County Health Management Committee, and attach the inspection report

The COC registration fee is KES 10,000. The licence fee is KES 10,000 for a Level 2 clinic and KES 20,000 for a Level 3 clinic.

### KMPDC health facility licence

A medical clinic needs a health facility licence from the [Kenya Medical Practitioners and Dentists Council (KMPDC)](https://kmpdc.go.ke/). You apply through the KMPDC Online Services Portal. The licence costs KES 15,000 for a medical clinic, and Form IX, the health facility registration form, costs KES 1,000. Inspection fees may be added, depending on the Sub-County Health Management Committee. The full approval process is in our guide to [KMPDC approval for a clinic](/how-do-i-get-approval-from-the-kmpdc-in-kenya/).

### What licensing adds up to

For a Level 2 clinic, plan on about KES 26,000: the KES 10,000 registration fee, the KES 15,000 licence and KES 1,000 for Form IX. For a Level 3 clinic the figure rises to about KES 36,000. The KES 1,000 business name registration sits on top of both.

## Licensing checklist

1. Register your business name with the Registrar of Companies through eCitizen. The fee is KES 1,000.
2. Get building approval permits from [Nairobi County](https://www.nairobi.go.ke/) before you build, at about 0.5% of the construction value.
3. Have your premises inspected by the Sub-County Health Management Committee and keep the inspection report, which the COC asks for. Inspection fees may apply.
4. If you are a clinical officer, apply to the [Clinical Officers Council](https://www.coc.go.ke/). You need a valid practising licence and a recommendation letter covering three years of supervised experience. Registration is KES 10,000, and the licence is KES 10,000 for Level 2 or KES 20,000 for Level 3.
5. For a medical clinic, apply to the [KMPDC](https://kmpdc.go.ke/) through its Online Services Portal: KES 15,000 for the health facility licence and KES 1,000 for Form IX.

## Running costs for the first three months

### Staff salaries

Salaries are the largest monthly line. Typical monthly pay in Nairobi:

| Role | Monthly salary (KES) |
| --- | --- |
| Clinical officer | 50,000 to 100,000 |
| Nurse | 30,000 to 60,000 |
| Receptionist or other support staff | 15,000 to 30,000 |

With one of each, the payroll is KES 95,000 to 190,000 a month. The budget above takes the low end: KES 285,000 for three months.

### Rent and utilities

Rent for a small clinic space runs KES 30,000 to 100,000 a month, depending on the area. Eastlands is at the cheaper end and Westlands at the dearer. Utilities, including the internet line, add KES 10,000 to 20,000 a month. Over the first three months, rent and utilities together come to KES 120,000 to 360,000.

### Equipment and supplies

Basic equipment such as an examination bed and a BP machine costs KES 100,000 to 500,000, and the budget uses KES 300,000. Consumables such as gloves and syringes run KES 20,000 to 50,000 a month, or KES 60,000 to 150,000 over three months.

### Insurance and the rest

Professional indemnity insurance costs KES 20,000 to 50,000 a year. Set aside another KES 50,000 to 100,000 at the start for signage and marketing. The budget above puts KES 70,000 against the two together.

## Where the budget moves

A specialised clinic needs more equipment than a general one. An eye clinic, for example, may add about KES 500,000.

Location changes the rent and the land. Rent in upscale areas such as Westlands can be double, and the land line alone ranges from KES 500,000 to KES 2 million.

## Keeping the opening budget down

- Start small. Open a small clinic first and expand as your patient base grows, which keeps the money at risk low.
- Choose an affordable area that patients can reach easily, such as Eastlands, and rent stays near the bottom of the KES 30,000 to 100,000 range.
- Ask more than one supplier to quote for equipment and consumables before you buy.
- Budget the licence fees up front. Paying the KMPDC or COC on time avoids penalties.
