This is the budget for a small general clinic in Mumbai: about 500 square feet in an affordable area, one consultation room, three staff, and enough money to run for three months after opening. The figures are planning ranges, not quotes. Fees change and this page can lag behind them, so get a written quote for each large line before you sign.

## The budget for a small clinic, line by line

A small general clinic in Mumbai comes to about ₹17.55 lakh, with three months of running costs included.

| Item | Cost (₹) | Basis |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Rent deposit | 1,00,000 | Between ₹50,000 in Thane and ₹3,00,000 in Bandra |
| Interiors | 3,00,000 | A 500-square-foot clinic |
| Furniture | 1,00,000 | A basic waiting room and consultation room |
| Technology | 2,00,000 | Computers and software, plus networking and CCTV |
| Basic equipment and sterilisation | 1,00,000 | BP monitors and thermometers, plus an autoclave |
| Diagnostic equipment | 2,00,000 | An ECG or blood testing machine for a general clinic |
| Licences and approvals | 1,00,000 | From a range of ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 |
| Medicines, initial stock | 75,000 | From a range of ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 |
| Staff salaries, 3 months | 2,70,000 | Three staff at ₹30,000 a month each |
| Rent, 3 months | 1,50,000 | ₹50,000 a month in an affordable area |
| Utilities, 3 months | 45,000 | ₹15,000 a month |
| Insurance and marketing | 70,000 | Liability cover plus launch marketing |
| Maintenance, 3 months | 45,000 | ₹15,000 a month |
| **Total** | **17,55,000** | About ₹17.55 lakh |

Read the total with two caveats. Most lines sit in the lower half of their ranges, so each is more likely to rise than fall when the quotes come in. And it leaves out surgical instruments, which add ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000 if you plan to do procedures.

## Premises and fit-out

### Rent deposit

The deposit is the first large cheque you write. It runs from ₹50,000 to ₹3,00,000 depending on the area: about ₹50,000 in an affordable area such as Thane, and up to ₹3,00,000 in Bandra. The budget above uses ₹1,00,000.

### Interiors

Flooring and ceilings, painting included, cost ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000. Air conditioning and lighting add ₹2,00,000 to ₹5,00,000 for a small clinic. For a 500-square-foot clinic, plan on about ₹3,00,000 for the interiors, which is the bottom of both ranges.

### Furniture

Seating and tables for the waiting room cost ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000. The consultation room, from the desk to the examination table, costs ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 as well. A basic setup for both comes to about ₹1,00,000.

### Technology and security

Computers and printers, with the clinic software, cost ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000. Internet and networking add ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000, and CCTV and alarms another ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000. Expect to spend around ₹2,00,000 in all.

## Medical equipment

### Basic equipment

Stethoscopes, BP monitors, pulse oximeters and thermometers cost ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 together. An autoclave for sterilisation costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,50,000. For a general clinic, the basic kit comes to about ₹1,00,000.

### Diagnostic equipment

An ECG machine or a blood testing machine costs ₹1,00,000 to ₹5,00,000, and a general clinic can plan on about ₹2,00,000 for diagnostics. A maternity clinic that needs an ultrasound machine should budget ₹5,00,000 to ₹25,00,000 for that machine alone.

### Surgical instruments

Surgical instruments and other tools cost ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000, depending on the services you offer. They are not in the total above, so add them if you plan to do procedures.

## Licences and approvals

Five pieces of paperwork stand between a finished clinic and the first patient. The ranges below are planning figures, not official fee schedules, so check each one with the office that issues it before you pay.

| Licence or approval | Cost (₹) |
| --- | --- |
| Doctor's registration with the NMC or the state medical council | 10,000 to 50,000 |
| Clinical establishment registration in Maharashtra | 10,000 to 50,000 |
| Fire NOC and environmental clearances | 50,000 to 2,00,000, by the size of the clinic |
| Local municipal permits and signage approval | 10,000 to 50,000 |
| Biomedical waste disposal authorisation | 10,000 to 50,000 |

For a small clinic, expect ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000 for all of it, and the budget above uses ₹1,00,000. Added up, the separate ranges in the table come to more than that, at the bottom end as well as the top, so get a quote for each one before you rely on ₹1,00,000. Get the fire NOC quote first, because that line alone can reach ₹2,00,000.

## Licensing checklist

1. Put ₹1,00,000 aside for licences and approvals at the start. Budgeting for them up front avoids penalties.
2. Check that each doctor who will practise at the clinic is registered with the [National Medical Commission (NMC)](https://www.nmc.org.in/) or the state medical council. This guide budgets ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 for the registration.
3. Register the clinic as a clinical establishment in Maharashtra, at ₹10,000 to ₹50,000.
4. Apply for the fire NOC and environmental clearances. They cost ₹50,000 to ₹2,00,000, depending on the size of the clinic.
5. Get the local municipal permits and signage approval, at ₹10,000 to ₹50,000.
6. Get biomedical waste disposal authorisation, at ₹10,000 to ₹50,000.
7. Keep every licence and approval in one file, with the date each one is due for renewal.

## Running costs for the first three months

The opening budget carries the first three months of running costs, so the clinic can pay its bills while the patient list builds. These are the six lines that make up that part of the table.

### Medicines

An initial stock of medicines costs ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000. A general clinic can plan on about ₹75,000.

### Staff salaries

Salaries for a small team run ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 per person per month. For three staff over the first three months that is ₹1,80,000 to ₹4,50,000. The budget above uses ₹30,000 a month each, or ₹2,70,000, and the team of three includes a salaried doctor. If you are the doctor and take no salary at first, one of the three drops out.

### Rent

Monthly rent runs from ₹50,000 to ₹5,00,000, so the first three months cost ₹1,50,000 to ₹15,00,000. The budget assumes a small clinic in an affordable area at ₹50,000 a month. Rent is the one line that can break this budget on its own: at the top of the range, three months of rent costs almost as much as everything else in the table put together.

### Utilities and maintenance

Electricity and water, with the internet line, cost ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 a month. Repairs to equipment and the building cost the same range again. The budget uses ₹15,000 a month for each, which is ₹45,000 each over three months.

### Insurance and marketing

Professional liability insurance costs ₹20,000 to ₹50,000 a year. Launch marketing, such as online ads and signage, costs another ₹20,000 to ₹50,000. The two together come to ₹40,000 to ₹1,00,000, and the budget puts ₹70,000 against them.

## Where the budget moves

Two things push a Mumbai clinic well past ₹17.55 lakh: the area and the specialty.

Rent in an upscale area such as Bandra can be double, and the deposit there can reach ₹3,00,000 against about ₹50,000 in Thane.

A specialised clinic such as cardiology can cost ₹50 lakh to ₹2 crore to open, mostly because of the equipment. A cardiology clinic with a cath lab runs ₹1 crore to ₹3 crore, and an MRI machine costs ₹5 crore to ₹7 crore. Most small clinics never buy either, but if your plan includes one, this budget is the wrong starting point.

## Keeping the opening budget down

- Start small. Open a small clinic first and expand as your patient base grows, which keeps the money at risk low.
- Look at Thane or Borivali before Bandra. Rent is the line that moves most, and an affordable area keeps both the deposit and the monthly rent near the bottom of their ranges.
- Ask more than one supplier to quote for equipment and supplies before you buy.
- Budget for licences and approvals up front. That avoids penalties.
