## What this page adds

This page covers only what is different for clinics in Nigeria. What Easy Clinic collects and how it protects that data are in the [Easy Clinic privacy policy](/privacy/), which applies here too. Where the two differ, [which document prevails for Nigerian clinics].

The law is the [Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf) (No. 37 of 2023), enforced by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC). The Commission's [General Application and Implementation Directive, 2025](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf) (GAID) explains how it applies the Act.

## Health data is sensitive personal data

The Act lists a person's health status as sensitive personal data ([section 65](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). Sensitive personal data may be processed only on one of the grounds in [section 30(1)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf). Two of those grounds are the patient's consent, given and not withdrawn, and processing for medical care by or under the responsibility of a professional who owes a duty of confidentiality.

## Your clinic and Easy Clinic

A controller that uses a processor must make sure the processor follows the Act, helps the controller honour patients' rights, keeps the data secure and says when it engages another processor ([section 29(1)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). Those measures include a written agreement between them ([section 29(2)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)).

## Registration with the NDPC

Data controllers and processors "of major importance" must register with the Commission ([section 44](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). The Commission's Guidance Notice, reproduced as Schedule 7 of the [GAID](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf), treats an organisation as being of major importance if it keeps a filing system for personal data and meets one of the Notice's tests. Two of those tests are processing the data of more than 200 people in six months, and working in one of the sectors the Notice lists, which include health.

The Notice puts organisations of major importance into three classes. Hospitals providing secondary or tertiary care sit in the middle class, and primary health centres in the lowest. Organisations in the two higher classes register once and file Compliance Audit Returns every year. Those in the lowest class renew their registration every year instead ([GAID Article 9](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf)).

## Data protection officer

A data controller of major importance must designate a Data Protection Officer with expert knowledge of data protection law ([section 32](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). The GAID adds that the officer's contact details must be published and given to the Commission ([GAID Article 11](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2025/07/NDP-ACT-GAID-2025-MARCH-20TH.pdf)).

## Breach timelines

A processor that becomes aware of a breach must notify the controller that engaged it ([section 40(1)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). The controller must notify the Commission within 72 hours of becoming aware of a breach that is likely to put people's rights and freedoms at risk ([section 40(2)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). Where the risk to a patient is high, the controller must tell the patient immediately, in plain and clear language, with advice on what they can do ([section 40(3)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). Controllers and processors keep a record of every breach ([section 40(8)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)).

If a breach touches your clinic's data in Easy Clinic, we tell you within [number of hours after Easy Clinic becomes aware of the breach], by [channel].

## Transfers outside Nigeria

Personal data may leave Nigeria only if the recipient is bound by a law, binding corporate rules, contractual clauses, a code of conduct or a certification that gives adequate protection, or if a condition in section 43 applies ([section 41(1)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). Those conditions include the patient's informed consent to the transfer, and a transfer needed to perform a contract with the patient ([section 43](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)). The controller or processor must record the basis for sending personal data abroad and why the protection is adequate ([section 41(2)](https://ndpc.gov.ng/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Nigeria_Data_Protection_Act_2023.pdf)).
