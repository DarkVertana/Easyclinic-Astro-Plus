The laws are the [Data Protection Act, 2019](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf) (No. 24 of 2019) and its [regulations of 2021](https://www.odpc.go.ke/data-protection-laws-kenya/), and the [Digital Health Act, 2023](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf) (No. 15 of 2023). The Digital Health Act says that anyone processing personal data under it must also comply with the Data Protection Act ([section 61](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)).

## Health data is sensitive personal data

The Data Protection Act lists a person's health status as sensitive personal data. It defines health data to include data collected when a patient registers for, or receives, health services ([section 2](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). Data about a patient's health may only be processed by or under the responsibility of a health care provider, or by a person bound by professional secrecy under any law ([section 46(1)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)).

The Digital Health Act puts consent on the healthcare provider. The provider must make sure it has consent to process sensitive personal data, except where a health service is provided for public health under the Public Health Act and in line with other statutory requirements. A patient may withdraw consent at any time by telling the provider ([section 31](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)).

## Registration with the ODPC

No one may act as a data controller or data processor in Kenya unless registered with the Data Commissioner, subject to thresholds the Commissioner sets ([section 18](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). The Registration Regulations exempt some small businesses ([regulation 13(2)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf)). That exemption does not cover processing for "health administration and provision of patient care": anyone processing personal data for that purpose must register, whatever its turnover ([regulation 13(4) and the Third Schedule](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf)). A certificate of registration is valid for 24 months ([regulation 9](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-REGISTRATION-OF-DATA-CONTROLLERS-AND-DATA-PROCESSORS-REGULATIONS-2021.pdf)).

## Data protection officer

The Act lets a controller or processor appoint a data protection officer in the cases it lists, which include core activities that consist of processing sensitive personal data ([section 24(1)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). The officer may be a member of staff, and a group of companies may share one officer if each can reach them ([section 24(2) and (3)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). Once an officer is appointed, their contact details must be published on the website and given to the Data Commissioner ([section 24(6)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)).

## Where data is kept and transfers outside Kenya

Personal data may leave Kenya only on a basis the Act lists: proof to the Data Commissioner of appropriate safeguards, or a necessity such as a contract with the patient or protecting someone's vital interests ([section 48](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). Sensitive personal data, which includes data revealing health status, may be processed outside Kenya only with the patient's consent and confirmation of appropriate safeguards ([section 49(1)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). A transfer made on the basis of safeguards must be documented with its date and time, the recipient, the reason and a description of the data ([regulation 41(2)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf)).

Two rules go further for health data. The General Regulations list "provision of primary or secondary health care for a data subject in the country" as a purpose of strategic interest to the state. Data processed for it must go through a server and data centre in Kenya, or at least one serving copy must be kept in a data centre in Kenya ([regulation 26](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/THE-DATA-PROTECTION-GENERAL-REGULATIONS-2021-1.pdf)). The Digital Health Act says personal health information may only be shared with anyone outside Kenya for the purposes of health tourism ([section 47](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)). ## Breach notification

Where personal data has been accessed or acquired by someone unauthorised and there is a real risk of harm to the patient, the controller must notify the Data Commissioner without delay, within 72 hours of becoming aware of the breach. It must then tell the patient in writing within a reasonably practical period ([section 43(1)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)). A processor that becomes aware of a breach must tell the controller without delay and, where reasonably practicable, within 48 hours ([section 43(3)](https://www.odpc.go.ke/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/TheDataProtectionAct__No24of2019.pdf)).

## What the Digital Health Act asks of software

The Act set up the Digital Health Agency, and one of its functions is to certify digital health solutions against best practice and standards ([section 6(m)](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)). A healthcare provider that delivers e-health services must make sure the platform it uses can work with the integrated health information system that the Agency runs ([section 43(1)(f)](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)). The Cabinet Secretary is to require every health data controller and processor to report designated health data in approved formats and on approved platforms ([section 21(2)(g)](https://nhts.dha.go.ke/api/file-download/?filename=Digital+Health+Act+15+of+2023.pdf)).
