## What this page adds

This page covers only what is different for clinics in India. What Easy Clinic collects and how it protects that data are in the [Easy Clinic privacy policy](/privacy/), which applies here too. Where the two differ, [which document prevails for Indian clinics].

The law is the [Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) (No. 22 of 2023), with the [Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf). For a plain guide to what the law asks of any clinic, whatever software it uses, read [patient data privacy laws in India](/patient-data-privacy-laws-in-india/).

## When the Act applies

The Rules were published on 13 November 2025. A [commencement notification of the same date](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf) (G.S.R. 843(E)) brought the provisions that set up the Data Protection Board into force at once. It brings in most of the rest eighteen months later, in May 2027: the duties on notices, consent, security, breach reporting, children's data and grievances.

The master policy was written under section 43A of the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the 2011 SPD Rules. [Section 44(2)(a)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) of the DPDP Act removes section 43A from the IT Act, and the commencement notification brings that change in on the same eighteen-month date, so section 43A stays in force until then. ## Your clinic and Easy Clinic under the Act

The Act calls whoever decides why and how personal data is processed the data fiduciary ([section 2(i)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Anyone who processes personal data on a fiduciary's behalf is a data processor ([section 2(k)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

For patient records, Easy Clinic's role is [Easy Clinic's role for patient records under the DPDP Act: data processor for the clinic, data fiduciary, or both]. For the accounts of your doctors and staff, and for our own billing records, Easy Clinic's role is [Easy Clinic's role for clinic user and billing data].

Two duties follow when a fiduciary uses a processor. The fiduciary stays responsible for processing done on its behalf ([section 8(1)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). It may also use a processor only under a valid contract ([section 8(2)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)), and that contract must provide for reasonable security safeguards ([rule 6(1)(f)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). ## Consent and privacy notices

Where consent is the basis for using a patient's data, the request for consent must come with, or after, a notice. The notice says what data is used and why. It also tells the patient how to withdraw consent or use their other rights, and how to complain to the Data Protection Board ([section 5(1)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). The Rules add that the notice must make sense on its own and must list the data item by item ([rule 3](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

The fiduciary must give the patient the option to read the notice in English or in any language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution ([section 5(3)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Withdrawing consent must be as easy as giving it ([section 6(4)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

Consent is not the only basis. The Act allows personal data to be used without it to respond to a medical emergency that threatens a person's life or poses an immediate threat to their health, and to treat people during an epidemic or outbreak of disease ([section 7(f) and 7(g)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

## Children's records

A child under the Act is anyone under 18 ([section 2(f)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). A fiduciary must get verifiable consent from a parent before processing a child's personal data ([section 9(1)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

The Rules lift that duty for a clinical establishment or a healthcare professional, but only where the processing is limited to giving the child health services, to the extent needed to protect the child's health ([rule 12(1) and the Fourth Schedule, Part A](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).

## Questions and grievances

Every fiduciary must publish the business contact details of someone who can answer questions about how it processes personal data, or of its Data Protection Officer if it has one ([section 8(9)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf) and [rule 9](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). It must run a way to settle grievances ([section 8(10)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)) and publish how long it takes to respond, which may not be more than 90 days ([rule 14(3)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). A patient must use that route before complaining to the Data Protection Board ([section 13(3)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)).

## Breach notification

After a personal data breach, the fiduciary must tell the Data Protection Board and each affected person ([section 8(6)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). Under [rule 7](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf), each affected person hears without delay, in plain words, what happened and what they can do to protect themselves. The Board hears without delay, and gets a detailed report within 72 hours of the fiduciary becoming aware of the breach, unless the Board allows longer.

## Hosting and transfers outside India

The Act does not require all personal data to stay in India. The central government may restrict transfers to countries it names by notification ([section 16(1)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)), and any Indian law with stricter rules on transfers still applies ([section 16(2)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2024/06/2bf1f0e9f04e6fb4f8fef35e82c42aa5.pdf)). A fiduciary must also meet any requirements the government sets on making personal data available to a foreign state ([rule 15](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)). The Rules let the government require a Significant Data Fiduciary to keep specified personal data in India ([rule 13(4)](https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/53450e6e5dc0bfa85ebd78686cadad39.pdf)).
